Event-study: ICE enforcement and foreign-born women's childcare employment, by setting

Foreign-born women's childcare employment, by setting
Event-study estimates around Trump's January 2025 inauguration. Click a setting to highlight its 95% confidence interval.
Each point is the estimated change in employment probability for foreign-born women per 1 SD increase in state-level ICE enforcement intensity. Month 0 is February 2025, the first full month after Trump's inauguration; month -1 (January 2025) is the omitted reference period. The earliest bin consolidates all leads at month -9 or earlier. Shaded band = 95% confidence interval for the highlighted setting.
Source: Aslim, Currie, Herbst & Tekin (2026), based on Current Population Survey and Deportation Data Project, September 2023–September 2025.
